## Textos apresentados

### TEXT I — The Growing Importance of Reliable Communication Networks

Communication networks have become essential to modern industries. From energy production to transportation systems, companies increasingly depend on digital infrastructure to monitor operations, exchange information and make decisions.

However, the expansion of connected systems has also created new challenges. Although technological advances have improved efficiency, they have introduced vulnerabilities that organizations cannot afford to ignore.

A single network failure may interrupt critical services, resulting in financial losses and operational delays. Therefore, many companies are investing in redundant communication paths, cybersecurity measures and continuous monitoring tools.

Nevertheless, technology alone cannot guarantee reliability. Skilled professionals, regular maintenance and effective planning remain fundamental to preventing failures and responding to unexpected events.

As industries become more interconnected, the ability to maintain secure and reliable communication systems will play an increasingly important role in their success.

### TEXT II — The Challenges of Renewable Energy Integration

Renewable energy sources, such as solar and wind power, have expanded significantly over the past decade. Governments and companies have invested in these technologies to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and diversify their energy supplies.

However, renewable energy generation is often dependent on weather conditions. Solar panels produce less electricity on cloudy days, while wind turbines require adequate wind speeds to operate efficiently. This variability creates challenges for electricity grids, which must continuously balance supply and demand.

Energy storage systems can help address these difficulties by storing surplus electricity and releasing it when production decreases. Although battery technology has improved considerably, the costs of large-scale storage remain a concern for many energy providers.

Furthermore, upgrading existing infrastructure is essential to accommodate a growing share of renewable energy. Without appropriate investments in transmission networks and grid management systems, the transition to cleaner energy sources may face significant obstacles.

Despite these challenges, renewable energy is expected to play a central role in future energy systems, provided that technological development and infrastructure investment continue.
