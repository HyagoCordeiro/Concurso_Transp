### Texto I — Why Predictive Maintenance Is Not Just About Sensors

Oil transport companies increasingly rely on digital sensors to monitor pipelines and pumping stations. These devices collect information about pressure, temperature and vibration, allowing engineers to identify potential problems before equipment fails.

However, technology alone cannot guarantee operational safety. A sudden pressure change, for instance, may indicate a leak, but it could also result from a routine operational adjustment. Engineers must therefore interpret the available information carefully.

Although automated systems can detect unusual patterns faster than humans, their effectiveness depends on accurate data and appropriate decisions.

Companies that invest heavily in monitoring equipment but neglect employee training may find that their technological improvements deliver fewer benefits than expected.

Ultimately, the purpose of predictive maintenance is not simply to collect more data, but to transform information into timely and reliable decisions.

### Texto II — Building More Resilient Energy Networks

As energy systems become increasingly interconnected, ensuring the reliability of critical infrastructure has become a major challenge. Power grids, telecommunications networks and fuel transportation systems depend on one another to maintain essential services.

In recent years, companies have invested in backup communication links and more sophisticated monitoring systems. Nevertheless, extreme weather events and cyberattacks continue to threaten operational continuity.

Experts argue that infrastructure resilience should not be measured solely by a system's ability to resist disruptions. Equally important is how quickly it can recover after an incident.

For instance, a telecommunications network that temporarily loses connectivity but restores its services within minutes may be considered more resilient than one that remains unavailable for several hours.

Although investments in redundant infrastructure may increase initial costs, they can significantly reduce financial losses and operational risks in the long term.
