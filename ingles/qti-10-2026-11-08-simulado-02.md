## Questões

1. **Phrasal verbs.** In the sentence “The meeting was called off due to unexpected technical problems”, the expression *called off* can be replaced, without changing the meaning, by:
A) postponed; B) scheduled; C) canceled; D) conducted; E) announced.

2. **Conjunctions.** “_____ the equipment was expensive, the company decided to purchase it because of its reliability.”
A) Despite; B) Although; C) Therefore; D) Unless; E) Moreover.

3. **Present Perfect.** Choose the grammatically correct sentence.
A) The engineers have finished the inspection yesterday.
B) The engineers has finished the inspection.
C) The engineers have finish the inspection.
D) The engineers have already finished the inspection.
E) The engineers have been finish the inspection.

4. **Relative Pronouns.** “The report, _____ was submitted yesterday, contains important recommendations.”
A) which; B) who; C) whose; D) whom; E) where.

5. **False Cognates.** In the sentence “The project will eventually be completed”, *eventually* means:
A) eventualmente, de vez em quando; B) provavelmente; C) imediatamente; D) ocasionalmente; E) finalmente, após algum tempo.

6. **Conditional Sentences.** “If I were responsible for the project, I _____ additional safety measures.”
A) will implement; B) would implement; C) had implemented; D) have implemented; E) implement.

7. **Quantifiers.** “Only _____ employees attended the training session, so the instructor decided to schedule another one.”
A) much; B) little; C) a little; D) a few; E) any.

8. **Passive Voice.** Choose the correct passive form of “The technicians will replace the damaged cables tomorrow.”
A) The damaged cables will be replaced tomorrow.
B) The damaged cables will replaced tomorrow.
C) The damaged cables are been replaced tomorrow.
D) The damaged cables have replaced tomorrow.
E) The damaged cables would replacing tomorrow.

9. **Connectors.** “The new software significantly improved performance. _____, it reduced operational costs.”
A) However; B) Nevertheless; C) Furthermore; D) Although; E) Despite.

10. **Reported Speech.** Choose the correct reported speech form of “The supervisor said, ‘I can solve the problem.’”
A) The supervisor said that he can solved the problem.
B) The supervisor said that he will solve the problem.
C) The supervisor said that he had solve the problem.
D) The supervisor said that he could solved the problem.
E) The supervisor said that he could solve the problem.

## Gabarito comentado

1. C — *Call off* = cancelar; *postpone* = adiar.
2. B — *Although* introduz contraste seguido de uma oração completa.
3. D — Present Perfect: *have/has* + particípio; *already* é compatível.
4. A — *Which* retoma *report* em oração explicativa.
5. E — *Eventually* = finalmente / após algum tempo (falso cognato).
6. B — Second Conditional: *if* + Simple Past; *would* + verbo-base.
7. D — *A few* acompanha substantivos contáveis plurais.
8. A — Voz passiva no futuro: *will be* + particípio passado.
9. C — *Furthermore* acrescenta informação.
10. E — No discurso indireto tradicional, *can* → *could*, seguido de verbo-base.

Gabarito: **C, B, D, A, E, B, D, A, C, E**.
